Understanding BFRBs

You’re not alone. And you’re not broken.

Body-focused repetitive behaviours (BFRBs) affect an estimated 1 in 20 people. They’re not habits you can simply “stop.” They’re deeply ingrained responses to stress, boredom, and emotional overwhelm. Most people who have them carry them silently for years.

The first step isn’t willpower. It’s awareness.

What makes UrgeWise different

Every step forward counts.

The battle isn’t just about stopping — it’s about catching urges before they become incidents. UrgeWise rewards you for both, because awareness is the first step to change.

Log an urge instead of acting on it? That’s a win. When you’re brave enough to log an incident? That’s an awareness victory. When you go 6 hours without any major incidents? That’s another win, automatically tracked by the app.

Everything about this app is designed to keep you motivated to monitor and manage your impulse control disorder over time. Stack enough wins and you may start to feel — for the first time in a long time — that you are actually on the road to recovery.
